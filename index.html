About The Dish

African salad (locally known as Abacha) is one of the meals mostly preapared and enjoyed by the Ibo speaking peoplnigeria. its native taste is so distintive, making delicacy to be enjoyed by all. It is usually served as a dish in special event such as traditional weddings, buceremonies, anniversaries or the visit of a special guestis best served with palm wine, a local drink tapped from trees, but you can also enjoy with any chilled drink of choice.

Ingredient

  • 300g Abacha(dried shredded cassava)
  • 100g Ugba(Ukpaka)
  • 200ml palm oil
  • 2 spoonfull of pounded crafish
  • 1 teaspoonfull of smoothly grounded potash
  • 1 cube of seasoning
  • 1 large (or 2 small onion)
  • Chilli pepper/ any hot pepper (to taste) salt
  • 100g of finely chopped Garden egg leave
  • Boiled Dried fish or Stockfish
